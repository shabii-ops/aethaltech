About Aethel Tech

An open phishing detection system developed for the hackathon, focusing on transparent heuristic scoring and actionable guidance.

Modern social engineering and phishing attacks rely heavily on emotional coercion and lookalike domains that bypass standard static keyword filters. Aethel Tech combines lexical URL analysis, targeted brand lookalike verification, and natural language urgency scoring to detect deceptive patterns in real time.

Brand Typosquatting Detection

Catches lookalike subdomains and mimicry targeting major payment, banking, and tech services (PayPal, Chase, Apple, Binance, etc.).

Bare IP Destination Routing

Flags raw IP addresses used in URLs to circumvent transparent domain registration transparency.

Disguised Executable Binaries

Identifies dangerous double-extension files (like .pdf.exe) disguised as standard corporate invoices or contracts.

Social Engineering Urgency

Scores psychological pressure tactics such as artificial deadlines, account suspension threats, and immediate legal warnings.

Credential Harvesting Traps

Detects explicit solicitations for sensitive credentials, OTP tokens, passwords, and cryptocurrency recovery seeds.

Shannon Token Entropy

Measures character randomness and information entropy to uncover encoded or obfuscated query parameters.
